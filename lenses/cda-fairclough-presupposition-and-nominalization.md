# CDA Fairclough Presupposition and Nominalization

## Trigger

Invoked when a frame or propaganda audit needs to inspect grammatical mechanisms that smuggle assumptions into discourse. The host mode supplies the text; the lens supplies a Critical Discourse Analysis scan focused on presupposition, nominalization, passivization, and agency deletion.

## Core Structure

Critical Discourse Analysis treats grammar as a site where social assumptions and power relations can become naturalized.

1. **Presupposition.** The text treats a contested claim as already accepted.
2. **Nominalization.** Processes are turned into nouns, hiding who acted or how change occurred.
3. **Passivization.** Actions are described without agents: "mistakes were made."
4. **Agency deletion.** Responsibility disappears through grammar, abstraction, or institutional voice.
5. **Lexicalization.** Word choice sorts actors into moral or political categories before argument begins.

## Application Steps

1. Identify sentences carrying important assumptions or responsibility claims.
2. Mark presupposed content: what must be accepted for the sentence to work?
3. Find nominalizations and rewrite them as verbs with actors.
4. Find passive constructions and ask who did the action.
5. Identify lexical choices that frame actors or events.
6. State what the grammar makes visible, invisible, natural, or contestable.

## Detection Signals

- A text makes a controversial premise feel already settled.
- Responsibility is hard to locate.
- Abstract nouns replace processes or actions.
- Institutional language seems neutral while guiding blame or legitimacy.
- Propaganda analysis needs a linguistic mechanism rather than only thematic interpretation.

## Critical Questions

- What is being presupposed rather than argued?
- Which process has been turned into a thing?
- Who disappears when the sentence is rewritten passively?
- What actor or institution benefits from the grammatical framing?
- Would the claim feel different if rewritten with active verbs and named agents?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Grammar scavenging** - Detection: every passive or noun is treated as manipulative. Correction: focus on load-bearing grammar.
- **Intent overclaim** - Detection: grammar is treated as proof of conscious deception. Correction: state discourse effect, not mind-reading.
- **Mechanism omission** - Detection: CDA is named but no sentence-level mechanism is shown. Correction: quote or paraphrase the grammatical form.
- **Context stripping** - Detection: sentence-level analysis ignores genre, audience, or institutional setting. Correction: relate grammar to discourse context.
