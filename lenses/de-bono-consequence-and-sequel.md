# de Bono Consequence and Sequel

## Trigger

Invoked when a mode needs to trace consequences across time rather than stop at immediate effects. The host mode supplies the action, event, or proposal; the lens supplies de Bono's Consequence and Sequel scan.

## Core Structure

Consequence and Sequel separates what happens next from what follows later.

1. **Immediate consequences.** Direct effects likely to appear quickly.
2. **Short-term sequels.** Follow-on effects as actors respond.
3. **Medium-term sequels.** System adjustments, incentives, dependencies, and adaptations.
4. **Long-term sequels.** Path dependence, normalization, lock-in, cultural change, or accumulated side effects.
5. **Positive, negative, and uncertain tracks.** Each time horizon can contain all three.

## Application Steps

1. State the action or change being analyzed.
2. Identify immediate direct consequences.
3. Trace short-term reactions by affected actors.
4. Trace medium-term system adaptations.
5. Trace long-term path dependence or second-order consequences.
6. Mark confidence and leading indicators for the most important sequels.

## Detection Signals

- A proposal is defended by immediate benefits alone.
- Delayed costs or benefits may dominate.
- Actors will adapt after the first effect.
- The analysis needs time-horizon discipline.

## Critical Questions

- What happens immediately?
- What do affected actors do next?
- What system behavior changes after adaptation?
- What long-term path does this make more likely?
- Which consequence is plausible but uncertain enough to monitor?

## Common Failure Modes

- **First-effect fixation** - Detection: only direct consequences are named. Correction: force short, medium, and long horizons.
- **Linear chain fantasy** - Detection: sequels ignore actor adaptation. Correction: ask who reacts and how.
- **Certainty overreach** - Detection: long-term effects are stated as facts. Correction: mark confidence and indicators.
- **Consequence sprawl** - Detection: every remote possibility is included. Correction: prioritize plausible, material sequels.
