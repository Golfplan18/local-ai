# Arrangement schemas — ordered slot-lists

*Each genre selects one schema. A schema is an ORDERED list of slots plus what goes in each — never a one-word label. The assembly step injects the chosen schema at the produce and format steps. Schemas are defaults with permission to deviate when the content plainly demands it; lead each slot with its point.*

## answer-first   (conversational)
direct answer → the why → any caveat. Give the answer in the first sentence; explain after; flag a caveat only if it changes the answer.

## motivation-first   (explainer)
hook → plain statement of what it is → why it matters to the reader → how it works → honest caveats → one-line takeaway. Earn the reader's attention before the mechanism.

## problem-rationale   (technical)
problem → decision or claim → rationale → alternatives considered → tradeoff → open problems. Every component earns its place by solving a named problem; the open-problems slot is required.

## goal-steps   (how-to)
goal or end-state → prerequisites → ordered steps → verification → troubleshooting. State the end-state first so the reader knows where the steps lead.

## inverted-pyramid   (journalism)
one-sentence lede (the outcome) → essential who / what / when / where → supporting detail in descending importance → background last. Most important fact first; nothing withheld for suspense.

## bottom-line-up-front   (business)
bottom line or recommendation → the two or three reasons → options and risks → the ask or next step. The decision-maker can stop after the first line and still know what to do.

## problem-recommendation   (white-paper)
problem → why it matters → analysis and evidence → options considered → recommendation → implications. Authoritative; the recommendation follows visibly from the evidence.

## thesis-evidence   (academic)
context or gap → thesis → method → evidence → discussion → limitations → conclusion. Hedge claims to the strength of their evidence; the limitations slot is required.

## defined-terms   (legal / regulatory)
defined terms → enumerated provisions → conditions and exceptions → cross-references. Unambiguous; each term used exactly as defined.

## benefit-led   (marketing / persuasive)
hook → the reader's benefit → proof → objection handled → call to action. Benefit before feature; the values floor blocks any claim you cannot support.

## reference-lookup   (reference / documentation)
what it is → usage or signature → parameters → examples → edge cases and errors. Optimized for the reader who already knows what they are looking for.

## scene-reflection   (narrative / personal essay)
opening image or scene → the tension or question → development → reflection → the turn or insight. Concrete before abstract; let the meaning surface from the particulars.
