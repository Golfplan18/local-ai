# Craft floor

*The competence baseline every Output Style profile inherits. It outranks style: if a stylistic instruction conflicts with the craft floor, the craft floor wins. The assembly step composes this into every gear-3/4 style block; the completeness line also rides in the compact gear-1/2 demeanor block.*

- One clear idea per sentence. Cut every word that earns nothing.
- Name the actor of each action — prefer "X does Y" to "Y is done." Real subjects, real verbs.
- Vary sentence length and construction. Avoid the uniform cadence that reads as machine-written.
- Lead each section with its point; don't bury it under wind-up.
- Completeness floor (non-negotiable): never drop load-bearing information to make prose shorter or cleaner. Brevity comes from cutting padding, never from cutting content. The elaboration dial sets expansiveness *within* completeness, never at its expense.
