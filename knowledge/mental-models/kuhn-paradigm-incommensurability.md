# Kuhn Paradigm Incommensurability

## Trigger

Invoked when worldview-cartography analysis needs to map why two frameworks cannot be compared by a neutral vocabulary alone. The host mode supplies the worldviews, paradigms, or conceptual schemes; the lens supplies Kuhn's paradigm and incommensurability frame.

## Core Structure

Kuhn argues that scientific communities work within paradigms: shared exemplars, standards, methods, problems, and meanings. During paradigm conflict, terms, evidence, and standards may not line up cleanly across communities.

1. **Paradigm.** Shared problem-field, methods, exemplars, and standards.
2. **Normal practice.** Puzzle-solving inside the paradigm.
3. **Anomaly.** Persistent trouble the paradigm does not handle well.
4. **Crisis.** Accumulated anomalies undermine confidence.
5. **Paradigm shift.** A new framework reorganizes problems, standards, and meanings.
6. **Incommensurability.** Rival paradigms may use terms, values, and evidence differently enough that translation is partial.

## Application Steps

1. Identify each worldview's central exemplars, standards, and problem-field.
2. Identify key terms that do not translate cleanly.
3. Identify what counts as evidence or success inside each worldview.
4. Map anomalies each worldview sees in the other.
5. Distinguish disagreement over facts from disagreement over standards.
6. Produce a translation map and note irreducible tensions.

## Detection Signals

- Participants use the same words with different meanings.
- Each side sees the other's evidence as irrelevant or malformed.
- The dispute persists after factual clarification.
- Standards of success, rigor, or explanation differ.
- A worldview map needs to show translation limits.

## Critical Questions

- What is each paradigm's exemplar case?
- Which terms shift meaning across paradigms?
- What counts as good evidence for each side?
- Which anomaly is visible only from one paradigm?
- Is the disagreement factual, methodological, evaluative, or semantic?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Relativism shortcut** - Detection: incommensurability is treated as "anything goes." Correction: map translation limits without denying comparison.
- **Neutral-language fantasy** - Detection: one paradigm's vocabulary is treated as neutral. Correction: mark whose terms are being used.
- **Anomaly inflation** - Detection: any problem becomes a crisis. Correction: distinguish ordinary puzzles from paradigm-threatening anomalies.
- **Total untranslatability** - Detection: partial translation is ignored. Correction: identify shared terms and bridge concepts.
