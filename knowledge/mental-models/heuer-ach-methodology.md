# Heuer ACH Methodology

## Trigger

Invoked when a competing-hypotheses mode needs a structured method for comparing explanations and resisting confirmation bias. The host mode supplies the question, hypotheses, and evidence; the lens supplies Analysis of Competing Hypotheses.

## Core Structure

ACH is a matrix-based method for comparing multiple hypotheses against evidence. Its key discipline is to seek disconfirmation and diagnostic evidence rather than build a case for the preferred answer.

1. **Generate hypotheses.** Include plausible alternatives, not just the favorite.
2. **List evidence and arguments.** Include observations, absences, assumptions, and source caveats.
3. **Score consistency.** Evaluate each evidence item against each hypothesis.
4. **Identify diagnostic evidence.** Prioritize evidence that separates hypotheses.
5. **Refine hypotheses.** Remove, split, or add hypotheses as needed.
6. **Draw tentative conclusions.** Prefer the hypothesis with the least inconsistency, not the most confirmatory evidence.

## Application Steps

1. Define the analytic question.
2. Generate a broad hypothesis set.
3. Build an evidence-by-hypothesis matrix.
4. Mark each evidence item as consistent, inconsistent, not applicable, or unknown for each hypothesis.
5. Identify the most diagnostic evidence.
6. Reconsider hypotheses with high inconsistency.
7. State the leading hypothesis, rivals, confidence, and evidence that could change the judgment.

## Detection Signals

- Multiple explanations remain live.
- The analyst may be favoring the first plausible story.
- Evidence quality varies and needs explicit treatment.
- The output needs confidence and residual uncertainty.

## Critical Questions

- Were plausible alternatives generated before evidence was weighed?
- Which hypothesis has the fewest serious inconsistencies?
- Which evidence is most diagnostic?
- What assumptions are doing load-bearing work?
- What new evidence would most change the conclusion?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Favorite-first ACH** - Detection: alternatives are weak foils. Correction: generate serious rivals before scoring.
- **Counting evidence** - Detection: the winner has the most supporting items. Correction: prioritize inconsistency and diagnosticity.
- **Matrix theater** - Detection: the matrix is filled but does not change judgment. Correction: explain which cells moved the conclusion.
- **Closed-world error** - Detection: all listed hypotheses fail but no new one is added. Correction: reopen hypothesis generation.
