# Ordinary Language Philosophy Tradition

## Trigger

Invoked when deep clarification needs to dissolve confusion by inspecting how a term, distinction, or question works in ordinary use. The host mode supplies the concept or disputed wording; the lens supplies ordinary-language diagnostic discipline.

## Core Structure

Ordinary language philosophy treats many conceptual problems as arising from misuse, overextension, or abstraction away from the practices in which words have meaning.

1. **Use before essence.** Ask how the term is used before asking what it "really is."
2. **Language games.** Meaning depends on rule-governed practices and contexts.
3. **Ordinary contrasts.** Terms gain meaning from what they contrast with in use.
4. **Category mistakes.** Confusion arises when grammar invites the wrong kind of object.
5. **Speech acts.** Utterances can do things, not merely describe things.
6. **Misleading abstraction.** Philosophical puzzles often intensify when ordinary context is stripped away.

## Application Steps

1. Identify the troublesome term, distinction, or question.
2. Collect ordinary uses and contrast cases.
3. Ask what practical work the term does in context.
4. Check whether the dispute depends on shifting use across contexts.
5. Identify category mistakes or misleading grammatical forms.
6. Restate the issue in use-sensitive language.

## Detection Signals

- A concept feels profound but gets less clear as definitions become more abstract.
- Disputants use the same word in different contexts.
- A question may be malformed by grammar.
- The analysis needs clarification before theory-building.

## Critical Questions

- How is the word ordinarily used?
- What contrasts make the word meaningful?
- What practice gives the term its role?
- Is a grammatical form misleading us about the kind of thing involved?
- Does the problem remain after ordinary uses are separated?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Dictionary stop** - Detection: ordinary language is reduced to a definition. Correction: inspect use in context.
- **Anti-theory overreach** - Detection: ordinary use is treated as final truth. Correction: use it to clarify, not to forbid revision.
- **Context flattening** - Detection: different language games are merged. Correction: separate contexts and uses.
- **Trivialization** - Detection: clarification is treated as mere semantics. Correction: show how wording changes the problem.
