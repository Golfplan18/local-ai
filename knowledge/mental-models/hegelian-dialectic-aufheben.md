# Hegelian Dialectic Aufheben

## Trigger

Invoked when dialectical analysis needs to move beyond simple compromise or thesis-antithesis pairing. The host mode supplies the opposed positions or conceptual tension; the lens supplies the discipline of Aufheben: cancel, preserve, and lift.

## Core Structure

Aufheben is often translated as sublation. It has three simultaneous movements:

1. **Cancel.** The inadequate form of a position is negated.
2. **Preserve.** The truth or insight inside the position is retained.
3. **Lift.** The retained insight is re-situated at a higher or more comprehensive level.

This is not the formula "thesis, antithesis, synthesis" as a mechanical three-step. The analysis must show how the opposition arises from internal limits and how the next position preserves what was true in the opposed terms.

## Application Steps

1. State each position in its strongest form.
2. Identify the internal limit or contradiction in each position.
3. Identify what truth must be preserved from each.
4. Identify what must be cancelled or transformed.
5. Formulate a higher-order position that preserves and re-situates the truths.
6. Check that the result is not a mere compromise.

## Detection Signals

- Two positions are both partly right but mutually inadequate.
- A synthesis must preserve real insights from both sides.
- The user needs conceptual development rather than option averaging.
- A debate is stuck because each side exposes the other's limit.

## Critical Questions

- What is true in each position?
- What internal limit makes each position unstable or incomplete?
- What must be negated rather than merely balanced?
- What is preserved in the higher-order position?
- Does the result explain why the original opposition arose?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Compromise masquerade** - Detection: the result splits the difference. Correction: show cancel/preserve/lift.
- **Caricature antithesis** - Detection: one side is weakened to ease synthesis. Correction: steelman both positions.
- **Formula ritual** - Detection: thesis-antithesis-synthesis is performed mechanically. Correction: analyze internal contradiction.
- **Vague transcendence** - Detection: the synthesis sounds lofty but changes nothing. Correction: state the new conceptual structure.
