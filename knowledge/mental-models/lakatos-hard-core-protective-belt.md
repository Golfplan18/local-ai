# Lakatos Hard Core Protective Belt

## Trigger

Invoked when paradigm-suspension analysis needs to distinguish a framework's central commitments from adjustable auxiliary claims. The host mode supplies the theory, worldview, or research program; the lens supplies Lakatos's hard-core/protective-belt structure.

## Core Structure

Lakatos treats scientific theories as research programmes rather than isolated hypotheses.

1. **Hard core.** The central commitments the programme protects from direct refutation.
2. **Protective belt.** Auxiliary hypotheses, assumptions, measurements, and interpretations adjusted to handle anomalies.
3. **Negative heuristic.** Rules that tell practitioners what not to abandon.
4. **Positive heuristic.** Guidance for extending the programme productively.
5. **Progressive problemshift.** Adjustments predict novel facts or increase explanatory power.
6. **Degenerating problemshift.** Adjustments merely patch anomalies after the fact.

## Application Steps

1. Identify the framework or research programme.
2. Separate hard-core commitments from auxiliary claims.
3. List anomalies or pressures on the framework.
4. Identify how the protective belt is being adjusted.
5. Assess whether the adjustment is progressive or degenerating.
6. State what would have to change to suspend or revise the paradigm.

## Detection Signals

- A framework survives repeated anomalies through auxiliary explanations.
- Critics and defenders disagree over what counts as refutation.
- A worldview has load-bearing commitments that are not explicitly argued.
- The question is whether a paradigm is adapting or merely immunizing itself.

## Critical Questions

- What claim is treated as non-negotiable?
- Which auxiliary claim absorbs the anomaly?
- Did the adjustment predict anything new?
- Is the programme generating insight or only preserving itself?
- What evidence would force hard-core revision?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Auxiliary confusion** - Detection: a belt adjustment is mistaken for core revision. Correction: name core and belt separately.
- **Patch tolerance** - Detection: any adjustment is treated as healthy. Correction: ask whether it is progressive.
- **Premature falsification** - Detection: one anomaly is treated as destroying the programme. Correction: inspect the protective belt and rival programmes.
- **Immunization blindness** - Detection: endless patches are treated as sophistication. Correction: look for degenerating problemshift.
