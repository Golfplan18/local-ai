# Shell Scenario Method

## Trigger

Invoked when scenario planning needs disciplined alternative futures rather than forecasts. The host mode supplies the strategic question and uncertainty field; the lens supplies the Shell scenario method.

## Core Structure

Shell-style scenarios are plausible, divergent stories about external conditions that help decision-makers see uncertainty and test strategy.

1. **Focal question.** The strategic decision or uncertainty the scenarios must illuminate.
2. **Driving forces.** Social, technological, economic, environmental, political, and institutional forces.
3. **Predetermined elements.** Factors already in motion and relatively certain.
4. **Critical uncertainties.** High-impact uncertainties whose outcomes are not knowable.
5. **Scenario logics.** Coherent causal stories, often organized by two critical axes.
6. **Implications.** Strategic moves, signposts, and robustness tests across scenarios.

## Application Steps

1. Define the focal question and time horizon.
2. Identify driving forces and predetermined elements.
3. Rank uncertainties by impact and uncertainty.
4. Select critical uncertainties and build divergent scenario logics.
5. Name each scenario with a memorable title.
6. Test current strategy against each scenario.
7. Identify signposts indicating which scenario may be emerging.

## Detection Signals

- The future is uncertain enough that one forecast is misleading.
- Strategic choices must be robust across plausible futures.
- External forces dominate internal planning assumptions.
- The analysis needs signposts rather than predictions.

## Critical Questions

- What decision will the scenarios improve?
- Which uncertainties are both high-impact and genuinely uncertain?
- Are scenarios causally coherent or just mood boards?
- Do scenarios diverge enough to stress strategy?
- What signpost would show one future becoming more likely?

## Common Failure Modes

- **Forecast disguise** - Detection: scenarios are high/medium/low versions of one forecast. Correction: create distinct causal logics.
- **Axis laziness** - Detection: the 2x2 uses convenient but weak axes. Correction: rank uncertainties by impact and uncertainty.
- **No decision link** - Detection: scenarios are interesting but do not affect strategy. Correction: tie back to focal question.
- **No signposts** - Detection: scenarios cannot be monitored. Correction: define early indicators.
