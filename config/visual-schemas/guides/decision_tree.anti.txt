The canonical wrong output is a "skeleton tree": correct square/circle/triangle topology but every leaf has no `payoff` and every node reads EV=0 (the optimum-plus failure), OR a tree whose "Wait/Pilot" branch is flattened to a single terminal leaf instead of modeling the real second-stage decision (premium/optimum failures), OR rolled-back EVs that are arithmetically incoherent — a positive root sitting over only-negative leaves (qwen9b failure). It degrades into a flowchart-with-circles that names options but produces no folded-back value or optimal path. The one rule that prevents it: put a numeric `payoff` on EVERY terminal, hand-compute the rollback (chance = Σ p·child, decision = max child), and emit the resulting `optimal_path` + `root_ev_by_branch` in `semantic_description.type_specific` — the diagram exists to produce that answer, not to draw boxes.
