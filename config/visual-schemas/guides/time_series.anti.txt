The canonical wrong output is a bare 2-point raw line on a tiny y-scale (v:1,2) with no moving-average overlay, no event annotation, and no forecast uncertainty band — a generic illustrative S-curve that "over-tells" as a DECLINE (slope goes negative) rather than a deceleration (slope flattens toward zero but stays positive). It also frequently fails the envelope `oneOf` outright by supplying only `layer` and omitting the schema-required top-level `mark`+`encoding`. The one rule that prevents it: keep top-level `mark:{"type":"line"}`+`encoding.x/y` present, use real business magnitudes, and ALWAYS render the prose-prescribed marks — MA line, event `rule`+`text`, and (in forecast mode) the `errorband` — because the judge scores fidelity on whether the image contains the marks the text promised.
