The canonical wrong output is a sequence that degrades into a flowchart: a single column of process steps joined by plain arrows (or worse, decision diamonds), with no distinct named lifelines passing messages to each other — or a "happy path only" diagram whose conditional branches are described in the prose instead of drawn. A close second is the qwen9b failure: a ghost lifeline that dangles unused on the far right because a participant was introduced implicitly mid-diagram or under two different spellings. The one rule that prevents all of these: declare every participant once up front (`actor`/`participant X as Label`), reference only those aliases, and DRAW every branch as a Mermaid `alt`/`else`/`opt` combined fragment inside `spec.dsl` — never as prose.
