The canonical wrong output is a "skeleton with floating parts": one stock with an inflow and outflow whose labels are long phrases that overlap illegibly at the valves, plus a list of auxiliaries (rate, ARPA, etc.) that appear in `auxiliaries` but never in `info_links`, so they render as disconnected boxes doing nothing — exactly the minimal fixture's flaw (label "Bar chart…" boilerplate aside). It also degrades into a causal_loop_diagram when no quantity actually accumulates. The one rule that prevents it: every auxiliary must be wired by at least one info_link AND every flow label must be ≤3 words and unique on its stock.
