The canonical wrong output is a boxplot (box + whiskers) emitted when the prose recommends an ECDF or histogram — it is in the distribution family so it validates, but it answers the wrong question and contradicts the text, the highest-frequency judge miss. The two other degeneracies: a LINEAR-axis histogram of a heavy right-skewed quantity (the long tail vanishes against the right edge, a data-honesty failure) when the prose mandates log; and a blank white frame from empty/placeholder `data.values`. The one rule that prevents all three: the `mark` and value-axis `scale` MUST mirror what the prose prescribes — boxplot only when the text wants a quartile summary (never when it says ECDF/histogram), `scale.type:"log"` with `integrity_declarations.log_scale_base` set whenever the prose calls the distribution heavy-tailed/skewed, and `data.values` must always carry real non-empty observations.
