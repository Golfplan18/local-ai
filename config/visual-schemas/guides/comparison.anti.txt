The canonical wrong output is a STACKED bar chart (year on x, product segments stacked) — a valid render that directly contradicts the analytical recommendation because stacking gives every series except the bottom a floating baseline, making cross-product comparison impossible. Two near-misses degrade the same way: a flat ungrouped bar of all product×period values (recreates the interleaving the prose flags), and a degenerate single-series 4-bar chart that silently drops the period dimension. The one rule that prevents all three: encode BOTH categorical dimensions and keep every datum — cluster by one (`encoding.x`), separate the other side-by-side (`encoding.xOffset` + `encoding.color`), on a shared zero-baseline length scale. If you ever reach for `stack` or drop a dimension to "simplify," stop.
