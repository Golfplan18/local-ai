**Structural definition.** A stock-and-flow (XMILE/Vensim) diagram models *accumulation under conservation*. `stocks` are rectangles that accumulate a quantity; `flows` are valved pipes that move quantity at a `rate` between two endpoints; `clouds` are the boundary (infinite source/sink) outside the model; `auxiliaries` are intermediate variables/constants; `info_links` are dashed arrows carrying information (NOT material) from a stock/auxiliary into a flow valve or another auxiliary. CAUSAL family, specialized XMILE compiler, `mode_context:"systems-dynamics-structural"`, `relation_to_prose:"visually_native"` (the diagram IS the deliverable — stricter review).

**THIS vs the sibling it's confused with.** Use stock_and_flow when the question is about *levels and rates over time* (cash, inventory, headcount, customers — things that fill and drain). Use `causal_loop_diagram` when you only have signed influences and feedback loops with NO conserved quantity. If you can't name what accumulates and in what unit, you want a CLD, not this.

**The exact failure modes to defeat (judge ground truth):**
1. **Colliding inflow/outflow labels** — the #1 blemish across ALL renders. A stock with one inflow and one outflow draws two valves close together; long `flows[].label` strings overlap illegibly. FIX: keep every flow `label` SHORT (1–3 words: "Revenue Collected", "Operating Spend", "Churned"), never a sentence, and never reuse near-identical labels on the same stock.
2. **Orphaned/disconnected auxiliaries** — "most auxiliaries hang unconnected." Every entry in `auxiliaries` MUST appear as a `from` or `to` in at least one `info_links` edge. An auxiliary with zero info_links is dead chartjunk. Wire each one to the valve or downstream variable it actually feeds.
3. **No diagram emitted at all** — a lane "produced NO rendered diagram." For this visual the artifact is mandatory; emit the envelope, never ASCII-in-prose.

**Layout + labeling rules.** Each `flows[].from` and `.to` MUST resolve to an existing stock `id` or cloud `id` (define the cloud in `clouds`). Every stock needs ≥1 flow touching it. `info_links.from` ∈ stocks∪auxiliaries; `.to` ∈ flows∪auxiliaries — and they must form a DAG (no auxiliary cycle). Keep `unit` consistent: stock in `USD`, its flows in `USD/month`. Give richness via auxiliaries + dense info_links (the strongest peer had "the richest information-link wiring"), but keep ≤2 stocks and short labels for legibility. Populate `initial`, `rate`, and `expression` to make it a real model, not a skeleton.
RENDER LEGIBILITY: keep topology lean — ≤2 stocks, ≤4 flows, ≤4 auxiliaries — and prefer a left-to-right series (cloud→flow→stock→flow→cloud). Dense webs of info_links and many parallel flows crowd the fixed bands. (Known renderer quirk: a flow valve can sit ~6% over its target stock; a measure-and-pack layout fix is tracked separately.)
