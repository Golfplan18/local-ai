**Structural definition.** A 2x2 / scenario-planning matrix (DECISION family, semantic-JSON→SVG). Two orthogonal driving uncertainties become axes; their crossing yields four labeled quadrants TL/TR/BL/BR, each a coherent future. Optional `items[]` plot real actors/options as points in normalized [0,1]×[0,1] space. Required spec keys: `subtype` (`strategic_2x2`|`scenario_planning`), `x_axis`, `y_axis` (each {label, low_label, high_label, description?}), `quadrants` (ALL of TL/TR/BL/BR, each {name, narrative?, action?, indicators?}), `axes_independence_rationale` (HARD-REQUIRED, non-empty). For `scenario_planning` the integrity checker ALSO enforces a non-empty `narrative` on every quadrant.

**THIS vs siblings.** Use quadrant_matrix when two *independent* uncertainties define a possibility space. NOT `comparison`/`scatter` (those plot measured data, not conceptual futures). NOT `ach_matrix` (hypotheses×evidence grid). If the two axes are causally linked (one drives the other), the matrix is dishonest — that's what `axes_independence_rationale` exists to defend.

**Failure modes from judge notes (defeat these):**
1. **Overlapping point labels in top quadrants** — the dominant flaw across 3 of 4 lanes ("illegible glyph soup," items "collapse onto two overlapping points," "quadrant headers colliding with center axis text"). DEFEAT: give every item a DISTINCT (x,y); never stack items at quadrant centers/corners. No two items should share a row (y) or column (x) — keep ~0.1+ separation on at least one coordinate. Pull items off the dead-center (0.5,0.5) and off the axis midlines where header/axis text lives.
2. **Wrong content substituted** (qwen lane: plotted 3 invented "paths" instead of the 5 requested items) — DEFEAT: every entity named in the prose/request must appear as exactly one item; don't invent abstract A1/A2/A3 placeholders.
3. **Over-engineered prose for a simple ask** — keep narratives tight, one idea each.

**Layout + labeling rules.** Name all four quadrants with memorable, distinct nouns (the premium lane won on naming). Quadrant `name` is the headline; `narrative` is 1-2 sentences; `action` is the strategic move; `indicators` are 1-3 early-warning signals. Axis `low_label`/`high_label` must be concrete poles, not just "Low"/"High". `axes_independence_rationale` must argue the two axes decouple in *mechanism* (different drivers) and show all four corners are coherent. Populate `items` so they fan across quadrants with clean separation; add a `note` per item for tooltip context. semantic_description: all three levels present, short_alt ≤150 chars describing THIS matrix (name the axes + quadrants), never bar-chart boilerplate.
